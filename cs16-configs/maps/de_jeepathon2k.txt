Jeepathon2K by Jason Blum
Created for CS Beta 7.0
Released August 24, 2000

Background:
Ride the jeeps, plant the bomb, and
save some hostages! This classic was
originally created for CS Beta 7.0,
but was removed for the official retail
release of CS. Rescued from the archives,
it's here to be enjoyed again by old and
new players alike.
